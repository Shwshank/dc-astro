Delhi transport is not difficult. It is overwhelming.

Multiple systems exist at the same time — metro, autos, cabs, walking routes — and the confusion comes from not knowing **which one to choose in which moment**.

This guide is not about options. It is about clarity.

---

## The Rule of Movement

Use this simple framework:

- **Long distance → Metro**
- **Medium distance → Cab**
- **Short distance → Walk or Auto**

Avoid mixing decisions mid-journey. That’s where stress begins.

---

## Metro: Your Default Backbone

The metro is the most predictable system in Delhi.

Use it when:
- Distance is more than 5–6 km
- Traffic is visible or expected
- You want fixed pricing and zero negotiation

Avoid it when:
- You are carrying heavy luggage
- You are unfamiliar with interchanges during peak hours

---

## Cabs: Controlled Comfort

Use cabs when:
- You want door-to-door movement
- Weather is extreme (heat, rain)
- You are moving between unfamiliar areas

Apps to rely on:
- Uber
- Ola

Always confirm:
- Pickup location
- Payment method (cash vs app)

---

## Autos: Situational Use

Autos are useful but unpredictable.

Use only when:
- Distance is short
- You are comfortable negotiating
- You understand approximate pricing

If unsure → skip autos.

---

## What Creates Stress (Avoid This)

- Switching transport modes mid-trip
- Asking too many people for directions
- Deciding on the spot without a plan

Delhi rewards **decisiveness**, not exploration during movement.

---

## Cognitive Relief Strategy

Before stepping out, decide:

- Mode of transport
- Backup option
- Payment method

Once decided → do not re-evaluate unless necessary.

---

## Final Thought

Delhi is not chaotic. It is layered.

When you reduce choices, the city becomes predictable.

That is where movement becomes calm.
